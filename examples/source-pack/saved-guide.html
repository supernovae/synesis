Working with pinned evidence

Keep a reviewed source snapshot when sharing project knowledge between clients. A source checksum identifies bytes, while a project revision identifies the chosen snapshot. Neither a checksum nor a revision proves that instructions in a document are safe to execute. The client continues to own execution and approvals.
